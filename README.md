# Click Goblin

A lightweight, portable Windows auto-clicker. Dark interface, live speed controls, no installer.

[![Download for Windows](https://img.shields.io/badge/Download-Windows_EXE-a78bfa?style=for-the-badge&logo=github&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/930turbo/click-goblin/releases/latest/download/ClickGoblin.exe)

Download the EXE, open it, choose your speed, and press **F6** to start or stop.

- Set CPS or an interval; adjust speed without leaving your game.
- Choose any mouse button, hold time, countdown, and click or time limit.
- Optionally lock clicking to one app, pausing when you Alt-Tab.

**Default hotkeys:** F6 start/stop · F8 faster · F7 slower · F10 emergency stop. All are customizable.
